## Our Dual Approach

<!-- Write this section's text and bullets in Markdown here. -->

The dronehacks competition had a drone navigate autonomously through a map with nothing but a depth camera (Oak-D), a jetson nano, and a dream. The easy approach is to set waypoints and A* through the provided map... but we didn't fly across the country to do something easy. Not only did we implement a full reinforcement learning approach, but also a classical pipeline that used SOTA methods for robot navigation. So yes, we are a little crazy :)
